Privacy, clearly.

No account, advertising or cross-app tracking. Optional AI features send the information needed to create the reading, answer or audio you request.

Who provides Palmistra

The Palmistra iPhone app is provided by Douaa Eljebari. Contact us through the support page.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile, birth date and time, chosen birth city, palm photos, saved readings and matches, chats, wishes, stories, journal, favourite affirmations and settings are stored locally. Birth chart calculations, daily readings, the calendar and rituals run on your device. Your birth date, exact birth time and saved birthplace are not sent to our AI service; derived zodiac signs can be sent.

Optional AI processing

Before the first personal AI request, Palmistra explains the processing and asks you to Allow or choose Not now. Allow covers the AI features described below. You can change your choice in Me → Privacy & AI. Local features continue working if you decline.

  • Palm reading and Love Match: the palm photos and the hand structure and lines visible in them, hand, first names, derived Sun/Moon/Rising signs, focus areas and onboarding answers relevant to the reading. In a two-person match, a derived compatibility score and signs may be included. Only upload another person's photo with their permission.
  • Guide: your messages and recent conversation, accessible reading summaries, wishes, sky context and profile context.
  • Stories and chapters: your wishes or relevant journal entries, first name, signs and other context needed to write the requested story, report or cycle letter.
  • AI narration: the text to speak, which can contain your first name, wish or generated reading, and your selected voice. Cached audio replays locally.
  • Horoscopes: the sign and requested period.

Service providers and retention

Requests pass through our Cloudflare Workers backend to OpenAI. The backend code does not intentionally save palm photos, readings, messages or wishes. A random installation identifier accompanies requests for usage limits; configured usage counters expire after approximately 26 hours. Generic sign-based horoscopes may be cached for up to 40 days. Cloudflare processes network information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect its service.

OpenAI processes AI inputs and outputs. API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default. The app's backend requests that responses not be stored as application state. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring data for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions for legal obligations or safety needs. Read OpenAI's API data controls. Choosing Not now prevents new personal AI uploads; deleting local data does not immediately erase provider security logs.

Requests may include your first name together with your photos, derived palm structure, other content and installation code. We disclose these as data linked to you for app functionality, without advertising or tracking.

Apple services

Apple handles purchases and subscription records; we do not receive your payment card details. Searching for a birth city sends the typed search to Apple MapKit. Palmistra does not request your GPS location. Camera access supports palm capture, and hand detection runs on your iPhone. Photo-library add access saves share cards you choose to create. Optional notifications are scheduled locally.

Your choices

Edit your profile in Me, change AI consent in Privacy & AI, and erase local information with Me → Delete all my data or by removing the app. Manage camera, photos and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Apple manages subscription cancellation separately. For questions about access, correction, deletion or other rights that apply where you live, contact support without posting personal data publicly.

Support and this website

This site uses GitHub Pages and adds no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may process web request data to provide hosting. If you open an issue, GitHub processes your account and submitted information, and the issue is public. Read GitHub's privacy statement. Include only the technical information needed to resolve your request.

Children and changes

Palmistra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request their personal information. This page will be updated if processing changes; material changes will also be explained in the app where appropriate.
