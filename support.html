HERE TO HELP

A little guidance.

Help with readings, Premium, privacy and technical issues.

Contact Palmistra

For technical help, open a GitHub support request with your iPhone model, iOS and app version, what happened and what you expected.

Open a support request

GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not include palm photos, birthdays, personal readings, private chat messages, passwords or payment details.

Get a clear palm reading

Use bright, even light. Open your hand with fingers relaxed and slightly apart; keep your whole palm inside the outline. The camera captures automatically when the hand is clear. AI processing requires your consent and an internet connection.

Use Palmistra without AI uploads

Choose Not now when asked for AI processing. Your on-device sky, day reading, daily ritual and journal remain available. Review your choice in Me → Privacy & AI.

Restore Premium

Use Restore on the paywall or Me → Restore purchases, with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Cancel a trial or subscription

Open Apple subscription settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal or the trial ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Request a refund

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete local data

Use Me → Delete all my data. This erases your local profile, photos, readings, conversations and journal. See the privacy policy for third-party processing and retention.

Report an AI reply

Use Report this reply in the guide to hide it, then contact support. Describe the problem without posting private content. AI can make mistakes; readings are for entertainment and reflection.
